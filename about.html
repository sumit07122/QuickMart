About QuickMart & Project

Frontend Assignment Showcase
⚡ Campus Quick Commerce

Built for College Students, by Students

QuickMart is an instant 10-minute online grocery delivery concept created for GLA University students. Whether it's milk for morning tea, chips for evening group studies, or mid-night noodles during exam season, QuickMart delivers right to the hostel door.

Our Process

How 10-Minute Delivery Works

Four swift steps from clicking order to opening your hostel door.

📱

1. Quick Order

Student chooses groceries and taps checkout in under 10 seconds.

📦

2. 2-Min Packing

Dedicated dark store picker bags items within 120 seconds.

🛵

3. Electric Transit

Eco-friendly campus delivery partner speeds toward the hostel block.

🎉

4. Doorstep Handover

Fresh produce handed over safely at the student's hostel gate.

Technology Stack Used

Built strictly adhering to the requirements of the college frontend syllabus:

  • HTML5 (Semantic markup, meta tags, forms, tables) Core
  • CSS3 (Custom gradients, smooth scroll, hover animations) Styling
  • Bootstrap 5.3 (Grid, Navbar, Cards, Badges, Modals) Framework
  • Bootstrap Icons (Scalable SVG web icons) Icons
  • Vercel (Global edge deployment & caching) Hosting
